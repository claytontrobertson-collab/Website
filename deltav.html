Calculation method and limits

Orbitalis is an early-phase mission estimator. It uses circular, coplanar Hohmann transfers at each body's mean semimajor axis, then adds an inclination-change estimate. Parking-orbit departure and capture use patched-conic hyperbolic excess velocity. Moon transfers include the parent planet's gravity well.

Do not use this tool for flight operations. It does not model launch windows from live ephemerides, eccentric transfer optimization, finite burns, perturbations, gravity assists, atmospheric ascent, landing, boil-off, power-limited low-thrust spirals, or structural margins.

Rocket performance uses Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀/m₁), evaluated sequentially for each stage. The calculated vehicle capability is ideal vacuum performance. Fictional presets are deliberately labeled and are for comparison and play.
